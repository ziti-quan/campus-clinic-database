8并发安全：条件更新 + 行锁（建议实现）

把判断与扣减合并为一条带条件的 UPDATE，由 InnoDB 行锁保证不超卖
操作语句
扣减号源UPDATE schedule SET remaining = remaining - 1 WHERE D_name = ? AND visit_date = ? AND remaining > 0
扣减库存UPDATE medicine SET stock_quantity = stock_quantity - ? WHERE med_id = ? AND stock_quantity >= ?
